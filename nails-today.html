Where can I get my nails done today?

Short answerCheck the What's happening board in the My Nail Connection app. Nail techs post Available now when they have an opening, and you can post what you need, where and by when. Techs nearby reply Interested, and you get her profile with a Call, Text or Book button. It is free for clients.

Two ways to find a same-day opening

  • See who is open. A tech posts Available now with an end time and a note, and her work glows in the Gallery for clients looking right now.
  • Ask for what you need. Post your city, a photo if you have one, the date you need it by and how far you would travel. Techs reply Interested.

What happens when a tech replies

  • You get that tech's profile and her Call, Text or Book button, so you can see her work and set it up.
  • Each tech chooses how far she travels, 10, 25 or 50 miles or her whole state, and gets a notification when a request lands within reach.

Safe to use

  • Every post can be reported and any person blocked. Reports are handled within 24 hours.

Questions people ask

How do I find a nail tech with an opening today?

Open the What's happening board in My Nail Connection to see techs who posted Available now, or post your own request so nearby techs reply Interested.

Does it cost anything to post a request?

No. My Nail Connection is free for clients, with no booking fees.

Can I add a photo of the nails I want?

Yes. A request can include a photo, your city, the date you need it by and how far you would travel.

What if someone posts something inappropriate?

Every post can be reported or the person blocked, and reports are handled within 24 hours.
