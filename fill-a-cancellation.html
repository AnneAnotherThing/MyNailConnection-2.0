The moment it happens

  • Post Available now with an end time and a note. Your work glows in the Gallery and on the What's happening board.
  • Booked? Turn it off. Forget, and it ends at the time you set.

Clients already asking

  • Clients post what they need, where and by when. Tap Interested and they get your Call, Text or Book button.
  • Set your reach, 10, 25 or 50 miles or your whole state, and get a notification when a request lands nearby.

Questions people ask

Does Available now cost anything?

No. It is free, with no commission and no per-booking fee.

How long does it stay up?

Until the end time you set, or until you turn it off.

Can I reach clients who already want an appointment?

Yes. Tap Interested on their request and they get your profile and your Call, Text or Book button.
