Questions people ask

Is My Nail Connection free for nail techs?

Yes. It is free to join and free to stay. The only paid feature is My Nail Connection's own booking, which is free for your first month and optional after that.

Does My Nail Connection take a commission?

No. There is no commission and no per-booking fee, ever, and your clients are never charged to book you.

Do I have to stop using Instagram?

No. My Nail Connection is one more place clients find you, searchable by your look and how close you are.

How many photos can I post?

Up to 50 a month, free, and they never expire.

Is it only for nails?

Yes. It is built for nail techs specifically. Nails only.
