2.0 → 3.0
What changed, and why
Strategy talking points

The story of the rebuild.

Four moves took My Nail Connection from 2.0 to 3.0. Each one is a talking point, use them in order and the whole arc tells itself.

1
The rebrand — quiet luxury.
Off the busy pink look onto the Nagel board: white space, mauve, black, the hat-no-face mark, black buttons. Reads calmer, more premium, and consistent from the site through the app to this handout.
2
The free-booking pivot — no commission, ever.
Booking is free for techs, with no per-booking fee. Two paths, their call: keep the link they already use, or turn on in-app booking. Nothing to switch, no reason to say no.
3
Accounts that just work — softer sign-in.
Sign-up and the confirm / reset emails were rewired so a tapped link lands people inside the app, already signed in. Booking asks only for a name and a verified phone.
4
The data rescue — nothing was lost.
The old database looked dead, it wasn't, just a stale password. Every tech, photo, and record from 2.0 was recovered on 15 July 2026. The final step is restoring it into the new paid database.
Where things stand
●DoneRebrand shipped to the 3.0 preview, marketing + app, and the tech guide.
