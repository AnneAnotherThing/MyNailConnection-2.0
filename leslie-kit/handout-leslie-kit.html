Talking points for the session

The story of the rebuild.

Four moves took My Nail Connection from 2.0 to 3.0. Each one is a talking point, use them in order and the whole arc tells itself.

1
The rebrand — quiet luxury.
Off the busy pink look onto the Nagel board: white space, mauve, black, the hat-no-face mark, black buttons. Reads calmer, more premium, and it's consistent from the site through the app to this handout.
2
The free-booking pivot — no commission, ever.
Booking is free for techs, with no per-booking fee. Two paths, their call: keep the link they already use (Vagaro, their site), or turn on in-app booking. Nothing to switch, no reason to say no.
3
Accounts that just work — softer sign-in.
Sign-up and the confirm / reset emails were rewired so a tapped link lands people inside the app, already signed in, instead of dropping them on a dead page. Booking asks only for a name and a verified phone.
4
The data rescue — nothing was lost.
The old database looked dead, it wasn't. It was a stale password, not lost data. Every tech, photo, and record from 2.0 was recovered on 15 July 2026. The final step is restoring it into the new paid database.
Where things stand
●Done  Rebrand shipped to the 3.0 preview, marketing + app, and the tech guide.
●Done  Free-booking model and softer sign-in built. Old-database rescue complete (15 Jul 2026).
●Pending  Restore the rescued data into the paid database, then real-device tests: one photo upload, one SMS booking.
●Pending  Cutover: promote 3.0 to the main site and retire 2.0.
