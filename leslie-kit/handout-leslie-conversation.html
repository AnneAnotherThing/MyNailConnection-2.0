Vs. booking sites "Booking sites manage the appointment. MNC creates the client."
They start from the tech, we start from the work. They search a name and get a list; on MNC you search the nails, chrome french, almond, gel-x, and the tech comes attached to the set you fell for.
They're calendars with directories bolted on. Discovery is an afterthought. MNC is discovery-first, and hands the client to whatever booking the tech uses.
No commissions, no client poaching. MNC never sits between a tech and her client relationship, or her money.
No switching cost. She doesn't move her calendar or deposits. She adds the place clients find her.

The line: booking sites and MNC aren't competitors, they're two halves of the funnel. MNC fills the top.

If pricing comes up (3.0 model): Free to start, your first photo's on us. More photos are $1 each, or $5 for 10. Glow Up is $10.99/month for 25 feed photos a month. And booking is free with no per-booking fee, keep the link you already use, or turn on in-app booking. Their call.

The questions, with answers.

If you two can answer these to each other on camera, you can answer anyone.

"Instagram's free and everyone's there. Why pay you?"
Because free isn't free. Instagram costs hours of content a week for reach the algorithm may never give, to an audience that's mostly not local. MNC starts free too. Glow Up is less than one soda a week, and one new regular pays for years of it. The real question is what Instagram is already costing her.
"Chicken and egg, no clients without techs, no techs without clients."
True of every marketplace, so you win one metro at a time. Start dense in the West Valley. Every tech who moves her link-in-bio to MNC imports her own clients, and each of those discovers three more local techs. That's what this filming push is for.
"Doesn't booking outside the app add friction?"
Not anymore. In 3.0 a client can book right in the app, free, in two taps, or the tech's own link is right there if she'd rather keep her system, deposits and policies. Either way MNC takes no cut. We're not trying to eat her business.
"What stops Booksy or Instagram from copying this?"
Nothing stops features. They can't copy focus. Instagram will never rank nail photos by shape and material within 10 miles. Booksy's incentive is to charge for bookings, not open discovery. And we ship weekly, with founders feeding us wishes directly.
"Stolen photos, catfish portfolios, how's quality?"
Honestly: community plus accountability. Local density is self-policing, a tech claiming work in a small radius gets called out fast. Verification's on the roadmap. "We know, and here's how we think about it," not "it's solved."
"Is the market big enough? It's just nails."
"Just nails" is a multi-billion-dollar US industry, hundreds of thousands of mostly independent techs with zero marketing help, exactly who big platforms ignore. Works for nails, the playbook extends to lashes, brows, hair. Nails first, because that's the world Leslie knows.
