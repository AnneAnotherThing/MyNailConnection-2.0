The idea

Here's my thinking.

I think techs aren't downloading because nobody wants to commit to a whole new tool sight unseen. Download fatigue is real, and they already live on Pinterest, Instagram, and Facebook, where re-posting the same nail photos everywhere is basically a second job.

The flyer, thought out.

The one-liner: My Nail Connection: free booking for nail techs. No commission. No per-booking fee. Ever.

Headline options: “Still booking in your DMs?” · “Your work is your booking page now.” · “Free booking for nail techs. Yes, actually free.”

The pitch: My Nail Connection puts your work where clients are already looking. Post your sets, tag them by shape, type and style, and clients browsing the Gallery find YOU — then book you right in the app. Already booking on Vagaro or GlossGenius? Keep your link — MNC sends clients to it.

Bullets to pull from: free booking built in · your own link + QR for your station · your whole book in your pocket (requests, walk-ins, private client notes) · standing appointments for regulars · 50 free photos a month that never expire · Open Today for same-day fills · quiet blocking.

Safe to print: free forever · 0% commission · $0 per booking · $0 monthly · App Store + Google Play.

Don'ts: no “use it in your browser” (web is download-only now) · no email-signup mention (it's phone sign-in) · no SMS-reminder promises (reminders are push).

Flyer QR should point at: mynailconnection.com/?src=qr-flyer — scans get tracked.

Saving these on your phone

However you're holding your phone, here's how

iPhone

Tap an image to open it full size, then press and hold it and choose Save to Photos. For the flyer PDF or a video, tap it, then tap the share icon (the box with the arrow) and choose Save to Files. The full kit saves into your Files app.

Android

Tap any download button and the file drops straight into your Downloads folder. Tap the notification to open it. The full kit downloads exactly the same way.

Easiest of all: scroll to the bottom and tap "Download the full kit," it grabs everything in one go.
