First Things First

Build a profile that gets you found.

Why this matters

Clients browse MNC on a map. If you don't have an address, you are literally invisible on it, like a shop with no front door. A complete profile with a real photo also makes clients three times more likely to reach out.

1

Sign in and open your Tech Portal

Tap the person icon in the top right corner of the app. That's your home base, everything lives here.

2

Add your profile photo

Upload a clear photo of yourself or your salon's logo. Think of this as your first impression, clients decide within seconds whether they feel comfortable reaching out. Make it warm, bright, and you.

Good lighting, clear face or brand. No filters needed, real and professional wins every time.
3

Enter your full shop address

This is how you get pinned on the map. Be precise, include suite numbers if you work in a salon. If you're mobile-only, enter your general service area. No address = no map pin = clients can't find you nearby.

Mobile techs: tag yourself as Mobile in your profile and your street address is automatically hidden from public view. Clients see your service area and can still call or text, but your home base stays private. You can toggle this any time from the Dashboard under Address privacy.
4

Write your bio

A few sentences is perfect. Tell clients: how long you've been doing nails, what styles you love, and what makes your work different. Don't overthink it, write like you're texting a potential client. Something like: "10 years doing nails in Phoenix, specialty in nail art and gel extensions. My clients always feel like they left with something special."

5

Add your phone number

Clients can call or text directly from your profile. No messaging platform, no app, just your number. Add it so they have a way to reach you even if they don't want to book online.

Each phone number can only be used by one MNC account. If you get a "phone already in use" error at signup, it usually means you (or a previous account tied to that number) are already on MNC, use Sign in or reset your password rather than creating a duplicate.
6

Tap Save, you're on the map

Once saved, your pin appears on the map for clients in your area. You're officially visible. Now let's get your portfolio working for you.

1

Open your Tech Portal and tap "Upload Photos"

This opens your photo uploader. You can upload directly from your camera roll.

2

Lead with your single best set

Your first photo is free, so make it the one that stops a scroll. After that, think variety: different shapes, colors, and styles. A client looking for ombre should see ombre. A client looking for nail art should see nail art. Every photo you add widens the net.

Pro tip: Square or portrait photos look best. Clear, well-lit, no blurry shots. Natural light is your best friend.
3

Tag each photo before saving, this is critical

Tagging is part of posting now: every look gets tagged right in the upload flow, and it goes live in the Gallery the moment you tag it. Tags are how clients find you — an untagged photo is invisible to filtered browsing, which is why the app won't let one slip through.

03
The Most Important Step

Tag every photo. Every single one.

An untagged photo is invisible to most clients.

When clients use the browse filters, shape, material, style, only tagged photos show up. This is how the majority of clients browse. Skipping tags means your work never gets seen by the people most likely to book you.

Here's the deal: clients don't search for you specifically, they search for the look they want. "I want almond shape, gel, with flowers." MNC matches that search to photos with those tags. If your gorgeous floral set isn't tagged, it simply won't show up. Think of tags as the keywords that put your work in front of the right eyes.

Shape

Square Round Oval Almond Coffin Stiletto Ballerina Flare Squoval Lipstick Edge Arrowhead

Material

Gel Acrylic Gel-X Dip Powder Hard Gel BIAB Polygel Natural Press-On Gel Polish Shellac

Style

Nail Art French Ombre Glitter Solid Marble Floral Abstract Chrome Minimalist 3D Gems/Crystals Animal Print Holiday Geometric Baby Boomer

You can, and should, apply multiple style tags to one photo. That floral set? Tag it: Floral + 3D + Nail Art. The more accurate tags, the more clients find it.

1

Upload your photo

Tap "Upload Photos" from your Tech Portal and choose the image from your camera roll.

2

Select the shape

Look at the nail in the photo and pick the closest match. If it's somewhere between square and oval, go with squoval. Don't overthink it, just be accurate.

3

Select the material

What product is on the nail? Gel, acrylic, dip? You know your work, pick the right one. Clients filter by this when they need a specific service.

4

Select all the styles that apply

This is where you can tag multiple options. A photo can be Chrome + Ombre + Abstract all at once. The more accurate, the better. Clients searching for any of those terms will see your photo.

5

Save the photo

It's now live on your profile and searchable in the Gallery. Every photo with proper tags is working for you around the clock, getting shown to clients who want exactly what you do.

The golden rule:

If you can't answer "what shape, what material, what style?" for a photo, the client can't find it either. Always tag before you save.

Already uploaded? Use your My Gallery screen.

If you had photos on your profile before tagging existed, or you skipped tags in a rush, head to Tech Portal → My Gallery. Untagged photos appear full-size at the top with grouped tag chips (Shape / Material / Style); already-tagged photos collapse into a Tagged (N) drawer below. Tap chips and your changes auto-save about a second after the last tap. You can also tap any tagged thumbnail to re-edit. It's the fastest way to catch your whole portfolio up.

04
Get Them Booked

Turn on booking, free forever.

Why this matters

MNC books for you, and it costs nothing. A client finds your work, taps once, and lands in your calendar. No commission, no per-booking fee, no trial that runs out. Booking lives with MNC and stays free for every tech, forever. Set your services and your hours and you're taking appointments.

Already book somewhere else? Keep it. Paste your existing booking page link and MNC labels the button on your profile automatically. You can run MNC booking, your own link, or both:

Vagaro Shows "Book on Vagaro"
StyleSeat Shows "Book on StyleSeat"
Square / GlossGenius / Other Shows "Book Now"
You start bookable.

Choose in-app booking and MNC seeds a starter setup for you — one "Full set" at 90 minutes and $65, with Mon–Fri 9–5 hours — so clients can book you from minute one. The steps below are how you make it yours.

1

Add your services, with a price and a length

Full set, fill, soak-off, whatever you actually offer. Each one gets a duration and a price, and that duration is what MNC uses to work out which appointment times you can genuinely fit.

2

Set your weekly hours

Tell MNC the days and hours you work. Clients only ever see slots inside those hours. Add buffer time between appointments if you need a breather, and block off days you're away.

3

Choose how bookings land

By default a booking arrives as a request and waits for you to confirm. Prefer it hands-off? Flip on auto-confirm and the slot books itself. Either way you and your client both get a reminder the day before and again three hours out.

Two clients can never grab the same slot. MNC locks the time the moment one of them takes it.
05
Get Found First

Flip your availability. Jump to the front.

Why this is a secret weapon

Clients searching for a same-day appointment are ready to book right now. When you flip the availability toggle, you appear first in those searches. This is completely free and takes two seconds.

Open today

Show up first for clients searching for same-day appointments

Open this week

Appear in searches for clients booking within the next 7 days

1

Open your Tech Portal any time you have openings

You don't have to keep this on all the time. Just flip it on when you have open appointments, first thing in the morning, when a client cancels, whenever.

2

Flip "Open today" or "Open this week"

Use Right Now when you have same-day openings. Use This Week if you have spots in the next few days. You can turn both on if it applies.

3

Turn it off when you're booked up

There's no penalty for turning it on and off. The toggle is yours, use it like a "Open / Closed" sign in your window. Keep it honest so clients aren't disappointed when they reach out.

06
Your Regulars

Standing appointments. Your regulars, on autopilot.

Why this is the one to set up

Your every-three-weeks regular shouldn't have to rebook every three weeks. One forgotten rebook is a gap in your week and a client who drifts. Turn an appointment into a standing one and the chair stays held, automatically, without either of you thinking about it. This is the feature the paid booking tools charge you monthly for.

These are real appointments, not reminders.

A standing appointment books actual slots on your calendar, the same as any other booking, so nobody else can take that time. MNC keeps topping the series up so there's always another one on the books ahead of you.

How it works, in practice:

Every 1 to 4 weeks

Pick the rhythm that matches your client, weekly, fortnightly, every three weeks, monthly.

Booked, not pencilled in

Each one is a real slot on your calendar. The time is held, so nobody else can book over it.

Tops itself up

As each appointment passes, the next one gets added to the end. The series never quietly runs out.

One week off is just one week

Cancel a single appointment without touching the rest. Holiday weeks don't break the rhythm.

1

Open the appointment in Bookings

Bookings sits at the top of your Tech Portal. Tap any confirmed appointment with a client who comes back like clockwork.

2

Tap "Make this standing" and pick the rhythm

Choose every 1, 2, 3, or 4 weeks. MNC books the series out for you from there and keeps it topped up. That's the whole setup.

Do this for your handful of true regulars first. That's usually most of a predictable week, locked in, in about two minutes.
