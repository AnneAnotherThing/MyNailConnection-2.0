The strategy

Installs we can count.

The web-preview era is over — mynailconnection.com/app is now a download page, full stop. Every link and QR we put into the world lands people on it, and when the link comes from a tech's own Share My Profile button, the page shows her photo, her work, and her name with the store buttons right underneath. Her own little billboard.

And it's all measured: every visit is logged with where it came from, every store-button tap is counted, and Android installs attribute back to the exact link that drove them. When we spend money on ads later, we'll know what worked.

The pitch

My Nail Connection puts your work where clients are already looking. Post your sets, tag them by shape, type and style, and clients browsing the Gallery find you — then book you right in the app. No commission, no per-booking fee, and never a charge to your clients. Three months free, then $10.99/mo. Already booking on Vagaro or GlossGenius? Keep your link — MNC sends clients to it.

Bullets to pull from

  • Real booking, built in — clients pick a service, a day, a time. You confirm, or auto-confirm.
  • Your own link + QR — Share My Profile puts your page in your IG bio, your stories, or a QR at your station.
  • Your whole book in your pocket — requests and your next appointment greet you on your portal; walk-ins go on the calendar with Add Appointment.
  • Private client notes — polish colors, allergies, no-show history. Only you ever see them.
  • Standing appointments — your every-3-weeks regular is booked out automatically.
  • 50 free photos a month, forever — and they never expire. Your share of the Gallery grows with your photo count.
  • Open Today — one switch and same-day clients see you first.

Safe to print

Founding rate, locked for life 0% commission $0 per booking $0 monthly App Store + Google Play

Flyer QR should point at mynailconnection.com/?src=qr-flyer — scans get tracked.

Don'ts: no “use it in your browser” (the web is download-only now) · no email-signup mention (it’s phone sign-in) · no SMS-reminder promises (reminders are push notifications).
