Instagram recruitment posts copy ready

Eight organic posts for techs on the all-welcome, booking-optional vibe: come look around, show your work, get found, add your number, and turn on booking whenever or never. Two or three a week over a month. Lead with post 1 or post 4. Keep this run separate from the feature emails so the same techs are not hit twice.

1 · A home for your work

Your work deserves more than a grid that buries it by Friday. My Nail Connection is a home for nail artists: post your sets and get found by clients searching for exactly your style. Come look around, there is nothing you have to do today. Free to join, free to stay. Link in bio.

2 · You get found

Clients here do not scroll hoping to stumble on you. They filter by shape, type and style, and they find you. Every set you post is one more way to be discovered. Your gallery is free, always, and it never expires. mynailconnection.com

3 · Reach, your way

Add your number and clients can call or text you right from your profile. No new system to learn, no pressure. Just an easy way for the right people to reach you. You choose what shows. It is your profile. Link in bio.

4 · Booking, only if you want it

Already book through Vagaro, StyleSeat or GlossGenius? Add your link and a Book Now button shows up on your profile, and we send clients your way. No booking system yet? Try ours free for 30 days. Like it, keep it for $10.99 a month. Do not, no worries, everything else stays free. Your call, always.

5 · The part that never changes

No commission. No per-booking fee. Your clients are never charged to book you. You keep your prices, your money, your people. That is not going to change.

6 · Fill a cancellation

Chair open this afternoon? Flip Open today and your work glows for the clients looking right now. Free, on every plan. Your best advertising is the work you already did.

7 · Your work stays put

This is not a feed. Nothing autodeletes and nothing scrolls into the void. The set you did last spring is still working for you next spring. Post the whole portfolio, not the highlights. It is yours.

8 · Come see

If your best work lives on a feed that forgot it by the weekend, come see what a home for your work feels like. Look around, post a set or two, add your number. Turn on booking whenever, or never. Free to join, and most of it stays free. Link in bio.

Before you post

Six things that are true today. Everything in the kit sticks to them.

  • Free to join. A tech's profile, gallery, place in the Gallery and own booking link never cost anything.
  • The only paid thing is MNC's own booking engine: $10.99 a month, first month free, and the month starts when she turns booking on. No card to start.
  • No commission, no per-booking fee, and never a charge to her clients.
  • Photos are free, up to 50 a month, and they never expire.
  • Nails only.
  • Both stores: App Store and Google Play.

Never say: a founding rate or a lifetime price (retired in August) · "free booking" without naming the month · "use it in your browser" · anything about email sign-up, it is phone sign-in · that MNC is the only marketplace, it is not.
