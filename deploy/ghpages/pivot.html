The 3.0 Pivot

We're not selling a photo app. We're selling the end of DM booking. One page on what we learned, who we're for, and what we say now.

🌸 just for us, not linked anywhere 🌸

1What 2.0 taught us

2.0 asked techs to post photos to a brand-new room. Instagram already has their followers; a new app starts empty. "Post here too" felt like extra work with no payoff, and that's not a caption problem, it's structural. Clients won't browse an empty gallery, and techs won't fill a gallery nobody browses.

2.0 asked for the audience before offering the tool. 3.0 leads with the tool.

2Who we're actually for

Not "all techs." Three kinds, one target:

Salon-employed techs
Not our buyer

The front desk books for them. They don't choose the software.

Techs already on Vagaro, Booksy, GlossGenius
Later, not first

Switching is painful and we don't need them to switch. The bring-your-own-booking-link path lets them join for the gallery whenever they're ready.

DM-book techs
Our customer

Independents and home-based techs whose "booking system" is Instagram DMs, texts, and memory. They feel the pain every single day: the what-times-do-you-have back-and-forth, the no-shows, the double-books. This is who every post talks to.

3The product, in one line

"Still booking in your DMs?"

That's the pitch. Free is the price tag, not the value, Square has a free booking tier and these techs never adopted it, because those tools feel like salon enterprise software. What only we have is the whole stack together:

  • Free forever. Not a trial, not a tier. Booking never costs a tech anything.
  • Built only for nails. No spa bloat, no front-desk features, nothing to configure that a nail tech doesn't need.
  • Her work IS her booking page. A client taps the set she loves and books the woman who did it. Nobody else has this.

4The quietly brilliant part

The booking tool works with zero other users. A tech sets up her book tonight and sends her own link to her own clients. And every client who books her creates an account with us to do it.

Every tech who joins brings her own clients. The audience 2.0 couldn't build gets built as a side effect.

5So when do photos make money?

Later, and that's fine, our costs are close to zero while the base grows. Two things make the $1 photo an easy yes when the time comes:

  • The framing never changes: a photo here is her storefront sign, permanent, working while she sleeps. An Instagram post dies in the feed. Ours never expires.
  • The magic moment: the first time a stranger finds her in the Gallery, taps her photo, and books her. After that happens once, the photo stops being a cost and becomes the best ad money she's ever spent. Everything we do early should aim at making that moment happen for every new tech.

6What changes now

  • No countdown. A tool doesn't need a premiere, it needs a drumbeat. The blunt posts we built ("Free booking? Fine.", "One app instead of three? Obviously.") become evergreen, posted on rhythm, no clock, no pressure.
  • The call to action becomes "set up your free book." No waitlist, no list to join. When 3.0 is ready, techs go straight into signup.
  • New content that works: DM-pain posts, 30-second demo reels (client taps photo, books, done), before-and-after of a chaotic DM thread next to a clean booking page.

7The one number we watch

Not followers. Not signups. Activated techs: set up her services and hours, shared her link, took one real booking. That's a tech whose business now lives with us. We check it weekly and it tells us the truth.

8Two things to stay ahead of

  • "Can I take a deposit?" will be asked in week one, no-shows are a DM tech's biggest money pain. The answer for now: put your Venmo in the booking note. Real deposit support is on the roadmap and will be a big retention win when it lands.
  • "Free forever... sure." This audience has been burned by creeping fees before. Our transparency page is a weapon: booking is free forever because photos are how we make money. We say the whole business model out loud, competitors can't.
Where we stand

For nail techs still booking through their DMs, MNC is the free booking system built only for nails, where your work is your booking page. Clients tap the set they love and book you. Free forever, because we make our money when you choose to show more work, not when you book it.
