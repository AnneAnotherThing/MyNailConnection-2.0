I rent a salon suite. How do new clients find me?

Short answerIn a salon suite there is no front desk sending walk-ins your way, so new clients have to find you. Put your real work where people search by look and distance. A free My Nail Connection profile and gallery shows your sets to clients nearby, lets you post Available now when you have an opening, and keeps your own booking link. No commission and no per-booking fee.

Your gallery is your storefront

  • A suite gives you your own space, your own hours and your own prices. It also means every new client is yours to find.
  • When someone is choosing a new nail tech, her work is the first thing they want to see. Put it where they are looking.

What to set up

  • A free profile with your city, your styles and your sets. Photos are free, up to 50 a month, and they never expire.
  • Tag every set by shape, type and style, so clients filtering for that look find you.
  • Add your booking link if you use Vagaro, StyleSeat, GlossGenius or another app. A Book Now button sends clients straight to it.
  • Choose Hide my street address if you would rather not show your suite's address on your public profile.

Fill the gaps in your book

  • A client cancels? Post Available now with an end time, and your work glows in the Gallery for clients looking right now.
  • Clients post what they need and when. Reply Interested and they get your profile and your Call, Text or Book button.
  • Choose your reach, 10, 25 or 50 miles or your whole state, and get a notification when a request lands within it.

Questions people ask

Does My Nail Connection cost anything for suite techs?

It is free to join and free to stay. The only paid feature is My Nail Connection's own booking, which is optional and free for your first month.

Do I have to leave my booking app?

No. Add your booking link and keep using the app you have. My Nail Connection adds a place for clients to find you by your work.

Will my suite address show?

Only if you want it to. Turn on Hide my street address and clients see your city, not your street.

Does My Nail Connection take a commission?

No. There is no commission and no per-booking fee, and your clients are never charged to book you.
