How do I find a good nail tech near me in Phoenix?

Short answerLook at her real work before you book. My Nail Connection is a free app with a Gallery of real sets from nail techs around the Valley. Filter by shape, type, style and distance, save the looks you love, then book, call or text the tech right from her profile. Clients never pay a booking fee.

What to look for in a nail tech

  • Photos of her own work. Not stock photos or reposts. The sets she actually did tell you more than any review.
  • The look you want. If you want almond chrome, French, 3D or gel-X, find a tech whose gallery is full of it.
  • How far she is. You will be going back every few weeks, so close by matters.
  • An easy way to reach her. Book, call or text, whichever she offers, without hunting through DMs.

How My Nail Connection helps

  • The Gallery shows real nail work from techs near you. Filter by shape, type, style and distance.
  • Tap a set you love to open that tech's profile, see more of her work, and book, call or text her.
  • Save looks to show your tech, and favorite your go-to tech so she is one tap away.
  • Need nails soon? The What's happening board shows techs who posted Available now.

Where around Phoenix

  • Nail techs on My Nail Connection work all over the Valley, from Glendale, Peoria and Surprise to Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa and Gilbert, and in Phoenix itself.
  • Set your distance in the Gallery and you only see techs close enough to matter.

Questions people ask

Is My Nail Connection free for clients?

Yes. It is free for clients, always, with no booking fees.

Can I find a nail tech for a specific look?

Yes. The Gallery filters by shape, type and style, such as almond, chrome, French, 3D or gel-X, and by distance, so you see real work in the look you want.

Can I book a nail appointment today?

Often, yes. Techs post Available now on the What's happening board when they have an opening, and you can post what you need so techs reply Interested.

Is My Nail Connection only in Phoenix?

No. Nail techs join from all over the US, and the Phoenix area has the most of them so far.

Does My Nail Connection do hair, lashes or brows?

No. It is built for nails only.
