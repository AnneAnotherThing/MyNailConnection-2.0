Get found

Your gallery is not a scrapbook.
It is how clients find you.

Clients do not scroll MNC hoping to stumble on you. They filter. When they filter, only tagged photos come back, which means an untagged photo is a beautiful set nobody will ever see.

Free

One Gallery, filtered by style

Real sets from real techs, in one place. Clients filter by shape, type and style, and only tagged photos come back.

Do this first

Tag every photo

Tags are the whole mechanic. Tag five photos and you are in five searches you were not in before.

In My Gallery, untagged photos float to the top so you never have to hunt for them, and your changes auto-save as you tap.

Free

Photos never expire

This is not a social feed. Nothing autodeletes and nothing scrolls into the void. The ombre you did last spring is still working for you next spring.

Up to 50 looks a month, free on every plan.

No pay-to-rank

Presence tracks your work, not your budget

Your share of the Gallery grows exactly with your photo count. No boosting, no mystery math, and nobody can outspend you into invisibility.

Profile

A profile that gets you found

Shop name, full address, bio and a profile photo. Clients use the map to find techs near them, and no address means invisible on the map.

A face or brand photo makes clients three times more likely to reach out.

Privacy

Mobile techs stay private

Tag yourself Mobile and your street address is kept private automatically. Clients see your service area, not your home base.

Fill the chair

Had a cancellation?
Fill it today.

A last-minute no-show used to just be money gone. Now it is a toggle, it takes two seconds, and it costs nothing on any plan.

Free

Open today

Flip it and your work glows in the Gallery right when the clients who need nails today are looking. That empty two o'clock turns back into a booking.

Clients have their own Open today filter, so flipping it moves you straight into the view they are already using.

Free

Open this week

For the slower gaps that are not quite an emergency. Same toggle, wider window.

Always yours

No slow-patch penalty

You do not pay to stay active and you are never quietly demoted for a quiet month. You are active until you take yourself down.

Free

Booking engine

Standing appointments

Your every-three-weeks regular should not have to rebook every three weeks. Turn any appointment into a standing one and MNC books the whole series, every 1 to 4 weeks, topped up automatically so the chair is always held.

This is the part the paid booking tools charge you monthly for.
