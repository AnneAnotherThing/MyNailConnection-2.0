For nail techs

Six steps and you are live. She walks you through them.

Sign in with your phone number and a texted code, no password. The setup checklist lives on your Tech Portal until the last box is ticked. Your profile, your photos and your place in the Gallery are free, and photos never expire. Read the setup guide

  1. 01

    Tag every photo, get found for exactly that

    Shape, type and style, as you post. Clients filter by those and by distance, and only tagged photos come back. Older photos are easy to catch up in My Gallery: untagged ones float to the top. Up to 50 photos a month, and they never expire.

  2. 02

    Three doors on your profile

    Book, Call, Text. Already on Vagaro, StyleSeat or GlossGenius? Add your link and a Book button appears on your profile, and you pay nothing. Mobile tech? Your street address stays private; clients see your city and service area.

  3. 03

    Booking built in, if you want it

    Services with length and price, weekly hours with split shifts, time off, a buffer between appointments, minimum notice, auto-confirm or approve each request. Walk-ins and phone clients added by hand. Client reminders go out for you. No commission, no per-booking fee, and your clients are never charged.

  4. 04

    Standing appointments, set and forget

    Turn any appointment into a standing appointment, every 1 to 4 weeks, and the chair is held for your regular without either of you rebooking. Keep a private note on any client that only you can see.

  5. 05

    A daily "Working today?"

    At a time you pick, a reminder opens the Available now sheet. One tap and your work glows in the Gallery. Clients near you who post on What's happening reach you as a push, up to three a day, never overnight.

  6. 06

    Your own link and QR

    Tap Share my profile on your Tech Portal and your link goes in your bio, your stories, a text to a regular. Print the QR for your station: one scan lands on a page that is all you, with the app right there.

Joining is free and stays free. Booking through MNC is free for your first month, then $10.99 a month, and only once you turn it on. No contracts, no card to start. Cancel anytime; your gallery and your calendar stay put.

Questions

Asked a lot, answered plainly.

Is My Nail Connection free?
Mostly, yes. Clients use My Nail Connection completely free, with no booking fees ever. For nail techs, joining is free and stays free: your public profile, your gallery, your place in the searchable Gallery, up to 50 photos a month that never expire, availability toggles and your own booking link all cost nothing. The only paid feature is MNC's own booking engine, which is free for your first month, then $10.99 a month if you keep it.
Does My Nail Connection charge commission or booking fees?
No. My Nail Connection takes no commission and no per-booking fee, and your clients are never charged to book you. A nail tech keeps her full price. The only cost is the optional $10.99 a month for MNC's own booking engine, and even that is free for the first month.
How do nail techs get found on My Nail Connection?
By their work. A tech posts her sets and tags them by shape, type and style. Clients search the Gallery by those filters and by distance, so a tech shows up for the exact looks she does. Posting Available now on the What's happening board puts her work in front of clients looking right now, and clients post what they need there too, so techs can reply Interested.
What is the What's happening board?
A live board inside the app. Clients post what they need and when, with their city and an optional photo. Nail techs post Available now with an end time and a note, and can reply Interested to a client's request, which sends the client the tech's profile and her Call, Text or Book button. It is free on every plan, every post can be reported or the person blocked from inside the app, and reports are handled within 24 hours.
Can I use my own booking site with My Nail Connection?
Yes, and it is free. Already book through Vagaro, StyleSeat or GlossGenius? Add your link and a Book Now button appears on your profile, sending clients straight to your existing system at no cost. No booking system yet? Try MNC's own booking free for your first month, then $10.99 a month if you keep it. Run your own link, MNC booking, or just calls and texts.
How much does My Nail Connection cost for nail techs?
Joining and everything that helps you get found is free, forever: your profile, your gallery, your place in the Gallery, up to 50 photos a month, availability, and your own booking link. MNC's own booking engine is the only paid feature, at $10.99 a month after a free first month. No commission, no per-booking fee, and your clients are never charged.
How do I find a nail tech near me?
Open the map or browse the Gallery by style. You'll see techs near you, filter by nail shape, type and look, save the sets you love, and reach a tech by booking, call or text, however she prefers.
Is My Nail Connection live?
Yes. My Nail Connection is live and free to join for clients and nail techs, on the App Store and Google Play, and at mynailconnection.com.
