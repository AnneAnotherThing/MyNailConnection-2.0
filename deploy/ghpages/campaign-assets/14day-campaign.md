# MNC 3.0, 14-Day Launch Campaign
**Audience:** Nail techs, named out loud in every asset and caption.
**Voice:** Blunt, confident, a little sassy. Every day is a demand
granted: "Free booking? Fine." No vague teasers, techs get it straight.
Never "rebuilt" or anything implying 2.0 failed.
**Arc:** You asked → We built it → Your move
**CTA on everything:** Get on the list at www.mynailconnection.com
**Campaign tag:** `#MNC3` `#MNC30` `#MyNailConnection`

**Clock:** the campaign runs on days-out, not calendar dates. It starts
when Anne says go; post Day 14 that morning. Nothing below needs
editing when the date is chosen.

**Before Day 14 goes up:** the waitlist form must be live on
www.mynailconnection.com (pointed at the NEW Supabase project), or the
CTA sends techs to a page with no list. See the note at the bottom.

**Assets:** campaign-assets/countdown/ has, for every day below,
an ANIMATED page (dayNN.html: open in a browser, F11 full screen,
screen-record ~10s, tap to replay) and static PNGs (dayNN-story.png
1080x1920, dayNN-feed.png 1080x1350). Every asset carries a
FOR NAIL TECHS badge and the get-on-the-list CTA. Everything is zipped
for Leslie at campaign-assets/mnc-3-countdown-kit.zip.

## POSTING NOTES

- **Best times for nail techs:** Tue–Thu, 7–9am or 8–10pm (before/after their work day)
- **Format:** the animated pages recorded as reels beat static posts.
  Mirror each post in Stories with the countdown sticker on top.
- **Comments:** first comment on every post = the next day's line.
- **Pain always points OUTWARD** (juggling apps, paying to be seen,
  vanishing feeds), never at MNC.
- **Logo reveal:** the melt page shows Day 9 (or drop day) and the new
  mark owns every visual from there.
- **Free-for-everyone rule:** booking is free for EVERY tech, never
  "for new techs" only, and paid photos apply to uploads, never photos
  already posted.
