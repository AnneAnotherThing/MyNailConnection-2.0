# MNC Flyer Brief — Tech Perspective (2026-08-11)

Everything below is true in the shipping app (v3.0). Safe to print.

## The one-liner
**My Nail Connection: free booking for nail techs. No commission. No per-booking fee. Ever.**

## Headline options (pick one)
- Still booking in your DMs?
- Your work is your booking page now.
- Free booking for nail techs. Yes, actually free.
- Find the look. Find the tech. Book them. (client-facing tagline)

## The pitch (short paragraph)
My Nail Connection puts your work where clients are already looking. Post
your sets, tag them by shape, type, and style, and clients browsing the
Gallery find YOU — then book you right in the app. No commission, no
per-booking fee, no monthly charge. Already booking on Vagaro or
GlossGenius? Keep your link — MNC sends clients to it.

## Feature bullets (tech's-eye view)
- **Free booking, built in** — clients pick a service, a day, a time. You
  confirm (or auto-confirm). Done.
- **Your own link + QR** — tap Share My Profile and your personal page goes
  in your IG bio, your stories, or a QR at your station. One scan → your
  photo, your work, your book.
- **Your whole book in your pocket** — requests and your next appointment
  greet you on your portal; walk-ins and phone clients go on the calendar
  with Add Appointment.
- **Private client notes** — polish colors, allergies, no-show history.
  Only you ever see them.
- **Standing appointments** — your every-3-weeks regular is booked out
  automatically. The chair stays held.
- **Post everything, free** — up to 50 looks a month, free forever, and
  photos never expire. Your share of the Gallery grows with your photo
  count. Nobody can outspend you into invisibility.
- **Open Today** — flip one switch and same-day clients see you first.
- **Block quietly** — problem client? They never see an opening again, and
  they're never told.

## Numbers / claims that are safe to print
- Free forever for techs (booking AND photos)
- 0% commission, $0 per booking, $0 monthly
- 50 free photo uploads a month, photos stay up permanently
- On the App Store and Google Play (search "My Nail Connection")

## Call to action
**Download My Nail Connection** — App Store · Google Play
(For the flyer QR: use `https://mynailconnection.com/?src=qr-flyer` so scans
are tracked; the site routes them to the stores.)

## Don'ts
- Don't advertise a web/browser version — the web is download-only now.
- Don't mention email signup — it's phone-number sign-in.
- Don't promise SMS reminders — reminders are push notifications.
