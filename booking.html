For nail techs

No booking system yet? We do that too.

Short answerSimple booking: everything you need, nothing you don't. Clients see your open times on your profile and book. You get a notification and confirm it, or let it confirm itself. No commission, no per-booking fee, and never a charge to your clients.

Set up in a minute

  • Add one service, with its name, length and price, and your weekly hours. Both start filled in with a common setup you can change.

Then it runs itself

  • Bookings come in as requests you confirm, or confirm themselves if you prefer.
  • A notification lands the moment someone books, and moving an appointment is a tap that tells everyone.
  • Standing appointments hold real time on your calendar, and you can keep a private note on any booking.

Already have booking?

  • Keep it. Add your Vagaro, StyleSeat or GlossGenius link and a Book Now button sends clients there, free.

Questions people ask

How much is booking on My Nail Connection?

Your first month is free and starts when you turn booking on. After that it is $10.99 a month.

Is there a commission or a fee per booking?

No. No commission, no per-booking fee, and your clients are never charged to book you.

Do I have to use it?

No. It is there if you want it. Your profile, gallery and your own booking link are free either way.
