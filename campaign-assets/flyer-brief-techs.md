# My Nail Connection: flyer brief for nail techs

The one-liner: Booking built for nail techs. No commission. No per-booking fee. Ever.

Headlines to pull from
- Still booking in your DMs?
- Your work is your booking page now.
- First month free, then $10.99 a month.
- Find the look. Find the tech. Book them.
- A home for your work.

The pitch, in one paragraph
My Nail Connection puts your work where clients are already looking. Post your sets, tag them by shape, type and style, and clients browsing the Gallery find you, then book you right in the app. No commission, no per-booking fee, and never a charge to your clients. First month free, then $10.99 a month, and it starts when you turn booking on. Already booking on Vagaro, GlossGenius or StyleSeat? Keep your link, MNC sends clients straight to it, free. You only pay if you want MNC's own booking engine.

Claims you can print
- 0% commission
- $0 per booking
- $0 charged to clients
- First month free, then $10.99 a month
- 50 free photos a month, forever
- Your profile, gallery and own booking link are free
- App Store and Google Play

Never print
- "Use it in your browser": the web is a download page now.
- Anything about email sign-up: it is phone sign-in.
- SMS reminders: reminders are push notifications.
- "Free booking" without naming the month.
- "Forever" anywhere near the word booking.
- "No trial": the offer is a free month.
- A founding rate or a lifetime price. Retired 2026-08-17.

Design: Playfair Display for headlines, DM Sans for everything else. White first, ink #141317, mauve #A2636C, blush #F7E2E3. See BRAND-COLORS.txt in the brand kit.
