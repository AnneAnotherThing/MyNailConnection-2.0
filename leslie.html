How to run it

  • Story each morning, feed square the same day — anyone who catches only one still gets the build.
  • Save every story to a Highlight called 3.0. The curtain only reads as a sequence, and it's lost if they expire.
  • On launch day, post all six squares as one carousel. A week's story in a ten-second swipe.
  • Don't answer the price in the comments before launch day. Someone will guess, someone will argue, and that argument is the campaign working.

The pitch, in one paragraph

My Nail Connection puts your work where clients are already looking. Post your sets, tag them by shape, type and style, and clients browsing the Gallery find you — then book you right in the app. No commission, no per-booking fee, and never a charge to your clients. First month free, then $10.99/mo. Already booking on Vagaro or GlossGenius? Keep your link — MNC sends clients straight to it, free. You only pay if you want MNC's own booking engine.

Claims you can print

  • Founding rate, locked for life
  • 0% commission
  • $0 per booking
  • $0 charged to clients
  • First month free, then $10.99/mo
  • 50 free photos a month, forever
  • App Store + Google Play

Never print: “use it in your browser” — the web is a download page now · anything about email sign-up — it's phone sign-in · SMS reminders — reminders are push notifications · “free booking” without naming the month · “forever” anywhere near the word booking · and “no trial” — the offer is a free month.

Where the links go

Installs we can count.

The web-preview era is over — mynailconnection.com/app is a download page, full stop. Every link and QR we put into the world lands there, and when the link came from a tech's own Share My Profile button, the page shows her photo, her work and her name with the store buttons right underneath. Her own little billboard.

And it's measured: every visit is logged with where it came from, every store-button tap is counted, and Android installs attribute back to the exact link that drove them. When we start spending on ads, we'll know what worked.

Put ?src= on everything — the flyer QR points at mynailconnection.com/?src=qr-flyer, the story link at ?src=ig-story. Scans and taps get counted separately.
