**The deal, in plain words.**

**Booking is free. Full stop.**
From day one, clients can find you, see your work, and book you right
there. They keep booking you there, forever. Booking lives with MNC and
stays free for every tech. Not a trial. Not a starter tier. Free.

**Your first photo is free. Make it count.**
It's your storefront in the Gallery from the moment you join.

**So how do we make money? Photos.**
After your first, each upload is paid: $1 a photo, $5 for a ten-pack,
or Glow Up at $10.99 a month for 25 uploads. That is the entire
business model. We only earn when you choose to show more of your work.

**More photos, more presence. That's the whole algorithm.**
Your share of the Gallery grows exactly with your photo count. No
boosting, no pay-to-rank, no mystery math.

**Too busy to upload this month? Your old photos keep working.**
Every photo you've ever paid for stays up permanently, still pulling
clients while you're heads-down at the table. Instagram rents
attention. Here, you own a billboard.

**Joined before 3.0?**
Your first five photos stay free, always. That was the promise and it
stands.
