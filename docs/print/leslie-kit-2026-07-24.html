Where 3.0 stands

The new model is simple: booking is free for techs, forever. Photos are how MNC earns, they are paid, permanent, and they double as each tech's advertising in the one shared Gallery. The whole app wears the new look, black, mauve, and blush, with the hat lady as the mark.

Your new sign-in (the big change)

Passwords are gone. You sign in with your phone number and a text message:

Go to mynailconnection.com/v3/app and tap sign in.
Enter your mobile number and tap Text me a code.
Type the 6-digit code from the text and tap Verify.
That's it. You land on your own tech dashboard, photos, services, everything intact.
Your old email login still works if you ever want it: on the phone screen, tap "Have an email login? Use that instead." Same for every tech.

What this means for your techs

  • Every tech signs in the same way now, phone number, text code, done. No more forgotten passwords, no more reset emails.
  • Existing techs keep their whole profile, that is exactly what we are testing together today.
  • New techs can add an email on signup if they want one, clients never need one at all.

Your posting kit

The campaign kit is one zip file: mnc-3-countdown-kit.zip. Inside are the animated cards (open one in a browser, press F11, screen-record about 10 seconds, post) plus ready-to-post story and feed images and a captions file. The campaign runs as a steady drumbeat now, not a dated countdown, so post them in any order, whenever it suits you.
