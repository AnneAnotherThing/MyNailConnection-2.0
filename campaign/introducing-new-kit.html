Claims you can print

Everything above traces back to something live.

Checked against mynailconnection.com and the shipping app on 20 September 2026. If a number changes, change it here first and the rest of the kit follows.

Price

$10.99 a month for MNC's own booking engine. One flat price; nothing scales with how busy you get.

Free tier

Profile, gallery, place in the Gallery, own booking link and Open today are free forever if you book elsewhere or take calls.

Trial

First month free, no card. It starts when booking is turned on, not at signup.

Fees

No commission, no per-booking fee, and never a charge to clients. Cancel anytime; gallery and calendar stay put.

Photos

Up to 50 looks a month, free. They never expire and nothing autodeletes.

Ranking

Share of the Gallery grows with photo count. No boosting and no paid placement.

Standing appointments

Every 1 to 4 weeks, topped up automatically.

Profile photo

A face or brand photo makes clients three times more likely to reach out. This figure comes from the live site; confirm the source before using it in paid advertising.

Blockers, settle these before a single send

1. Email deliverability is not signed off. Every line of the Site Launch Checklist mail block is still open on the MNC punchlist: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, Resend domain verification, and a from-address on mynailconnection.com. Sending eight marketing emails from an unverified domain lands them in spam and spends sending reputation you cannot get back. This is the one hard stop.

2. "Founding tech, locked for life" has been removed from this kit. The live home page still prints it, but the punchlist records Anne's 2026-08-17 decision of no founder carve-outs. A lifetime price promise is not something to repeat across eight emails while the two sources disagree. Put it back everywhere if the live page is right; correct the live page if the punchlist is.

3. The home page does not say when the free month starts. The app stamps the trial when booking is switched on, not at signup. Emails 01 and 07 and social post 8 lead on that, so the home page needs the same sentence or a tech reads two stories in one week.

4. Open booking bugs. The punchlist flags listing_paused reading true for all 32 techs, and a RevenueCat reset that leaves Leslie paid but unbookable, both marked "before any booking push". This campaign is a booking push. Driving techs to turn booking on while those are live means they hit the bug, not the feature.

5. The waggle is not at 100%. Standing rule: nothing goes live until it is.
