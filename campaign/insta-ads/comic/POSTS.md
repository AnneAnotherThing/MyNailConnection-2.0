# The comic: posts for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok

Files: `mnc-comic-v2-1080.png` (square, the whole strip with the brand band),
`mnc-comic-v2-story.png` (9:16), and `panels/01.png` to `07.png` (six panels
plus a closing brand card, 1080 square each, for a swipe or slideshow).

The strip is a client story with a tech payoff, so it speaks to both. Each
caption below leads with the client and lands on the tech.

## Facebook

Post the square image. Facebook gives you room, so the caption can carry the whole pitch.

> Broke a nail and your nail lady is out of town? This is the whole reason My Nail Connection exists.
>
> Open the app, browse real sets from real techs near you, and tap the one whose work you love. Pull up her gallery. Call her, text her, or book her right there. No calling around, no waiting on replies.
>
> And if you are the tech with the empty chair: your work is your booking page now. Post your sets, flip Open today when you have a gap, and let the clients who are looking right now find you.
>
> Free to browse. Free to book. Nails only.
>
> mynailconnection.com

## Instagram

Post the seven panel cards as a carousel, in order, so the story reads one swipe at a time and ends on the brand card. The square works as a single post too.

> Oh no. Broke a nail. Nail lady out of town.
>
> Swipe. This is what My Nail Connection is for. Browse real sets from real techs near you, find the one whose work you love, and reach her right from her gallery. Book in the app, or call, or text.
>
> Techs: that empty chair is the other half of this story. Your work is your booking page. Post it.
>
> Free to browse. Free to book. Link in bio.
>
> #nails #nailtech #nailsofinstagram #nailinspo #brokeanail #nailappointment #naildesign #findyournailtech #supportsmallbusiness #mynailconnection

## TikTok

Two ways to run it. The photo-mode swipe is the easy one: upload `panels/01.png` to `07.png` as a photo post, pick a trending sound, and let people swipe through the story. If you want a video, hold each panel for about 1.5 seconds and the closing card for 3.

Caption (TikTok cuts long captions, so this one is short):

> POV: you broke a nail and your nail lady is out of town. There is an app for this. Browse real techs by their actual work, then book, call or text her from her gallery. Free to browse, free to book, nails only.
>
> #nailtok #nailtech #brokeanail #nailsoftiktok #nailappointment #naildesign #smallbusiness #mynailconnection

On-screen text for the first frame, if you add any: **Broke a nail. Nail lady out of town.**

## Notes

- No exclamation points and no em dashes anywhere, per house style. The bubbles in the strip follow the same rule.
- Panel 6 has the tech saying "Just filled my empty chair." In this strip it is the ending of one client's story, not a promise about the app, so it stays.
- Pinned comment idea, all three platforms: "Techs, your profile and gallery are free. The only thing you ever pay for is our booking tool, and you can use your own instead."
