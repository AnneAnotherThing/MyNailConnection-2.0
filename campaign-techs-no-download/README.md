# My Nail Connection, "No Download" Tech Campaign

A complete marketing kit aimed at nail techs, built to fix the download-fatigue problem by leading with the browser version at **mynailconnection.com/app** as the instant, no-download way to try it. The full apps on the App Store and Google Play stay in the picture as the next step, not something to replace.

**The story it tells:** reposting your work across Pinterest, Instagram, and Facebook is a second job. Post it once in My Nail Connection, get found by clients near you, always free. Try it in your browser in one tap, or get the full app on the App Store and Google Play.

All graphics use your real app screenshots (the inspo gallery, the browse view, and the map) in a bolder, higher-contrast version of your brand look (deep plum, coral, gold, Playfair italic accents).

## What is in here

| Folder | Files | Where to use it |
|---|---|---|
| `01-story-slides` | 6 PNGs, 1080x1920 | Instagram + Facebook Stories, post as a set or one a day |
| `02-feed-carousel` | 6 PNGs, 1080x1350 | Instagram + Facebook feed carousel (swipe post) |
| `03-one-pager` | PNG + print PDF | Print for salons, supply counters, beauty schools, hand-outs |
| `04-square-hero` | 1 PNG, 1080x1080 | Single hero post, Facebook, link previews, quick share |
| `05-video` | 2 MP4s | `story-reel` for Reels/Stories (vertical), `feed-reel` for feed (4:5), each about 13 seconds |
| `assets` | QR code | Branded QR to mynailconnection.com/app, drop into any print or slide |
| `captions.md` | Text | Ready-to-paste captions, hashtags, and a 1:1 outreach DM script |

## Suggested first two weeks

- **Day 1:** Post the feed carousel (`02-feed-carousel`) with the carousel caption from `captions.md`.
- **Day 2:** Post the story reel (`05-video/mnc-techs-story-reel.mp4`) with a link sticker.
- **Day 3 to 5:** Drip the 6 story slides one per day to Stories.
- **Ongoing:** Pin the square hero, print the one-pager for any salon or school you visit, and send the outreach DM to 5 techs whose work you already like.

## Notes
- Everything points to mynailconnection.com/app on purpose. That is the no-download hook to get techs in the door, and every piece also names the App Store and Google Play so the native apps still get promoted.
- The QR code in `assets` resolves to mynailconnection.com/app. Worth scanning it once to confirm before printing.
- Source HTML and the render script are kept separately and are not needed to use these files.

1. **Confirm the QR**, scan `assets/qr-mynailconnection-app.png` with your phone to make sure it opens mynailconnection.com/app cleanly.
