For Nail Techs

Your work deserves to be found.

Your booking profile is live the moment you join — free, forever. Photos are free too — every photo you add lands in the Gallery where clients browse by style, up to 50 a month. No slow patch penalty. You’re active until you take yourself down.

Read the complete setup guide →

01

Build a Profile That Gets You Found

Fill in your shop name, full address, bio, and a profile photo. Clients use the map to find techs near them, no address means invisible on the map. A face or brand photo makes clients three times more likely to reach out. Mobile tech? Tag yourself Mobile and your street address is kept private automatically, clients see your service area, not your home base.

02

Tag Every Photo , Critical

When clients filter by shape, type, or style, only tagged photos appear. An untagged photo is invisible to filtered searches, which is how most clients browse. Tag each photo with Shape, Material, and Style so the right clients find your work. The My Gallery screen makes catching up on older photos fast, untagged ones float to the top, and your changes auto-save as you tap.

03

Set Your Availability, Get Seen First

Flip Open today or Open this week from your Tech Portal. Clients searching for same-day appointments will see you first. This is completely free and takes two seconds.

04

Clients Book You Right in the App

Clients find your work in the feed and book you directly in the app — no third-party platform required. Add your phone number and they can call or text too. Already on Vagaro, GlossGenius, or StyleSeat? Add your booking link and a Book Now button appears automatically. Every path leads to you.

05

Post Updates. Stay Top of Mind.

Share availability windows, promos, and behind-the-scenes peeks using the New Post button. Posts appear on your public profile, another free way to stay visible with clients who already love your work. The board is intentionally one-way: no comments, no replies, no DM threads. Clients read the update and reach out on your terms, a call, a text, or a tap on your booking link.

06

Post Everything , Free

Every photo you post lands in the Gallery — and photos are free for every tech, up to 50 a month. Have a slow patch? You don’t pay to stay active. You’re active until you take yourself down.

Your work stays put. A portfolio that grows with you.

This isn't a social feed, nothing autodeletes, nothing scrolls into the void. Every photo you add is yours. It's your portfolio, your archive, your show-and-tell, the ombre you did last spring is still there next spring. Booking is free, and so are your photos — post your whole portfolio, up to 50 looks a month. Every photo compounds. Your work never disappears.

Free booking. Free photos, up to 50 looks a month. Free, forever.

No contracts, no commission, no fees for your clients, no monthly charge. The 50-a-month limit is only there to keep things running smoothly — almost nobody reaches it, and it refills every month.
