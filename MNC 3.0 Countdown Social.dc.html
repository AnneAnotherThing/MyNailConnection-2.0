How to run it
Post the story each morning and the feed square the same day, so anyone who only catches one still gets the build. Save every story to a Highlight called 3.0 — the curtain dropping is only legible as a sequence, and it's completely lost if they expire.
On launch day, post all six feed squares together as one carousel. Anybody who missed the week swipes the whole story in ten seconds and lands on Free. All of it.
Don't answer the price in the comments before launch day. Someone will guess, someone will argue, and that argument is the campaign working.
