2 · The launch letters

Two audiences, two letters

Letter A — Founding techs (loaded portfolios + lifetime free)

For the founders whose accounts + photos are already in MNC 2.0. Says “you’re free for life because you believed,” includes their starter password (MNC2026), CTA goes to the founders feedback page.

File: og17-launch/send-to-og17.html

Letter B — Original founders (no loaded photos, no freebies)

For folks who created an account in MNC 1.0 but didn’t carry through — including the ~5 emails captured by the pre-launch popup. Acknowledges them as founders, asks them to sign up fresh, gives the founders feedback link. No mention of any freebies.

File: og17-launch/send-to-original-founders.html

SMS for the OG founders

Three length variants in og17-launch/sms-to-ogs.txt — send the email first so the SMS reference to “letter in your inbox” is true.

How to send

Both letters use Resend merge tags — {{first_name}} for greetings and {{email}} in the sign-in callout. Drop them into a Resend template, attach your tech list, send.

3 · The founders feedback page

Where founders tell us what’s up

URL: mynailconnection.com/founders.html

A branded public page that asks founders three short prompts:

  • Something you’d add?
  • Something annoying?
  • Anything else?

At least one prompt is required. Submissions land in the founders_feedback table — you read them on your admin page (next section).

4 · Your admin home

Sign in with your MNC admin account

URL: mynailconnection.com/admin-feedback.html

One page, two tabs:

Feedback tab

Every submission from the founders page. Filter by category (Wishes / Annoyances / Other), hide-resolved toggle, click the checkbox to mark resolved as we work through them.

Tracker tab

Our shared post-launch enhancement list — for things you and I think of that aren’t coming from founders. Add an item with a title, optional details, and a priority (High / Med / Low). Each item shows who added it in a little badge, so we always know who’s noting what. Mark items done as they ship; reorder priority inline.

Heads up

The page is RLS-gated for admin role on public.users. If you sign in and see “Not authorized”, your auth account isn’t flagged as admin yet — ping me and I’ll fix it.

6 · When Apple drops — the moment-of routine

A short checklist for when approval lands

  • Send the OG letters via Resend. Founding techs first (Letter A), then non-loaded founders (Letter B).
  • Send the SMS to the OG founders 30–60 min after the email so the “check your inbox” reference is fresh.
  • Push notification to all existing app users: “We’re live on iOS too.”
  • Social blitz — Instagram, TikTok, the salons we know.
  • Watch the dollars — Stripe + RevenueCat for first paying techs.
  • Watch the feed — as OGs tag their photos, the feed populates and discovery starts working.
