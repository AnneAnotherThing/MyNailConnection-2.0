# MNC — Nail Techs · 30-second AI script

**Audience:** independent nail techs, salon-based techs, freelance nail artists
**Goal:** get them to download MNC + start their portfolio
**Hook angle:** *get on there first — early techs get seen first*
**Tone:** warm, confident, slight urgency. NOT salesy.

---

## Voice-over / on-camera dialogue

> Nail techs — listen up.
>
> Right now, clients in your area are scrolling for someone exactly like you.
> Browsing by style. Saving sets. Booking on the spot.
>
> The question is — are they finding **YOU**?
>
> My Nail Connection puts your portfolio right where clients are already looking.
> Built for techs. Free to start. No middleman.
>
> The early ones get seen first.
>
> Get MNC. Be one of them.

**Word count:** ~70 words · **Read length:** ~28-32 seconds at natural pace.

## Pacing + delivery notes

- **First line ("Nail techs — listen up.")** — slight pause before "listen up." Direct address. Establish: this is FOR you.
- **"are they finding YOU?"** — emphasize **YOU**. The question is the hook.
- **"The early ones get seen first."** — slow this line down a beat. It's the FOMO landing.
- **"Get MNC. Be one of them."** — confident, almost smiling. Not pushy.

---

## Variants to consider

**Tighter (15s, ~35 words) — for vertical IG/TikTok hook:**
> Nail techs — clients in your area are scrolling for someone exactly like you. Are they finding YOU? My Nail Connection puts your work where clients are looking. Free to start. Get MNC.

**Longer (60s, ~140 words) — for YouTube pre-roll or full-length pitch:**
> Add a "what makes MNC different" beat after "...where clients are already looking":
> *"It's not a booking app — your existing setup stays. MNC just gets your work in front of the people looking for it. Style-based discovery means clients find you by what you do, not where you are. Free to start with five portfolio slots. Glow Up adds five fresh photos every Sunday — so your work stays in rotation, every week, on autopilot."*
> Then continue with "The early ones get seen first..."
