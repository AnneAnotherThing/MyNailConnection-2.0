For Nail Techs

Your work deserves to be found.

Your booking profile is live the moment you join, free, forever. Your first photo is free, and every photo you add lands in the Gallery where clients browse by style. After that, photos are $1 each, $5 for ten, or Glow Up at $10.99 a month for 25 uploads. No slow patch penalty. You’re active until you take yourself down.

Read the complete setup guide →

01

Build a Profile That Gets You Found

Fill in your shop name, full address, bio, and a profile photo. Clients use the map to find techs near them, no address means invisible on the map. A face or brand photo makes clients three times more likely to reach out. Mobile tech? Tag yourself Mobile and your street address is kept private automatically, clients see your service area, not your home base.

02

Tag Every Photo, Critical

When clients filter by shape, type, or style, only tagged photos appear. An untagged photo is invisible to filtered searches, which is how most clients browse. Tag each photo with Shape, Material, and Style so the right clients find your work. The Tag My Photos dashboard makes catching up on older photos fast, untagged ones float to the top, and your changes auto-save as you tap.

03

Had a Cancellation? Fill It Today.

A last-minute no-show used to just be money gone. Now you flip Available Today and your work glows in the Gallery, right when the clients who need nails today are looking. That empty two o'clock turns back into a booking. Two seconds, no cost, on every plan. Flip Available This Week for the slower gaps.

04

Clients Book You Right in the App

Clients find your work in the Gallery and book you directly in the app, no third-party platform required. Add your phone number and they can call or text too. Already on Vagaro, GlossGenius, or StyleSeat? Add your booking link and a Book Now button appears automatically. Every path leads to you.

05

Standing Appointments, Set and Forget

Your every-three-weeks regular shouldn’t have to rebook every three weeks. Turn any appointment into a standing appointment and MNC books the whole series for you, every 1 to 4 weeks, automatically topped up so the chair is always held. Your regulars stop slipping through the cracks, and you stop chasing them. This is the part the paid booking tools charge you monthly for.

06

More Photos, More Presence, That’s the Whole Algorithm

Every photo you add lands in the Gallery, and your share of it grows exactly with your photo count. No boosting, no pay-to-rank, no mystery math. Nobody can outspend you into invisibility, because presence tracks your work, not your budget. Have a slow patch? You don’t pay to stay active. You’re active until you take yourself down.

Your work stays put. A portfolio that grows with you.

This isn't a social feed, nothing autodeletes, nothing scrolls into the void. Every photo you add is yours. It's your portfolio, your archive, your show-and-tell, the ombre you did last spring is still there next spring. Start with free booking and your first photo free. Ready to grow? Add photos one at a time for $1, grab 10 for $5, or go Glow Up at $10.99 a month for 25 uploads. Instagram rents attention. Here, you own a billboard.

Free booking, always. Add photos when you're ready to grow.

For Nail Addicts

Find the look. Connect with the tech. Obsess.

Stop scrolling Instagram hoping to find a local tech. My Nail Connection puts every talented artist near you in one beautiful place, searchable, filterable, and ready to book. Find the look, find the tech, book them — all in the app.

Browse by Style

Two ways to browse. Find a Tech mode shows real nail work near you, filter by distance, tap any photo to visit that tech's profile and book. Get Inspo mode is pure inspiration: open the Gallery up nationwide, heart looks you love, no distance filter.

Save Your Inspo

Heart any photo to save it to your personal inspo board. Bring it to your appointment and show your tech exactly what you want. No more lost screenshots.

Find Techs Near You

See nail techs on a map. Set your distance radius and browse by proximity, whether you want someone three blocks away or thirty miles out.

Favorite Your Go-To Tech

Star your favorite artists so they're always one tap away. See their latest work, check their availability, and tap to text, call, or book, all in one place.

Available Right Now

Need a same-day appointment? Tap Available Today to instantly see which techs are open today. No calling around, no waiting for replies, just reach out directly and get on their calendar.

Book Right in the App

Find the look, find the tech, book them — without leaving MNC. Prefer to call or text? Every tech lists their contact info too. Either way, no third-party platform to learn, no guessing how to reach them.
