What's the best app to find a nail tech near me?

Short answerIt depends on the job. If you already know your nail tech, use whatever she books through. If you are looking for a new one, use an app that shows real work first. My Nail Connection is a free, nails-only app: browse a Gallery of real sets by shape, style and distance, then book, call or text the tech. Clients never pay a booking fee.

Two kinds of apps

  • Booking and business apps such as Vagaro, StyleSeat, GlossGenius, Booksy and Fresha run a beauty pro's calendar, payments and reminders, and several also let you search for pros. They cover many kinds of beauty work.
  • A nails-only discovery app such as My Nail Connection is built for finding the right nail tech by her actual work, close to you, before you book.

What to look for when you choose a new tech

  • Real photos of her own work, in the look you want.
  • Filters for shape, type and style, not only a name search.
  • Distance, so she is close enough for every fill.
  • An easy way to reach her: book, call or text.

Where My Nail Connection fits

  • Nails only, so every photo in the Gallery is nail work.
  • Filter by shape, type, style and distance, and save the looks you love.
  • See who has an opening today on the What's happening board.
  • Free for clients, with no booking fees. A tech who already uses a booking app can add her link, and the Book Now button on her profile sends you there.

Questions people ask

Is My Nail Connection free for clients?

Yes. It is free for clients, with no booking fees.

Can I book a tech who uses Vagaro, StyleSeat or GlossGenius?

Yes, if she has added her booking link to her profile. The Book Now button sends you to her booking page, where you book the way you already do.

Is there an app just for nails?

Yes. My Nail Connection is built for nails only: nail techs and the clients looking for them.

Does it work outside Phoenix?

Yes. Nail techs join from all over the US, and the Phoenix area has the most of them so far.
