How to run it

Posting

  • Post the story each morning and the feed square the same day, so anyone who catches only one still gets the build.
  • Save every story to a Highlight called 3.0. The curtain dropping only reads as a sequence — it's lost completely if they expire.
  • On launch day, post all six feed squares as one carousel. Anyone who missed the week swipes the whole story in ten seconds.
  • Don't answer the price in the comments before launch day. Someone will guess, someone will argue, and that argument is the campaign working.
  • Best times for nail techs: Tue–Thu, 7–9am or 8–10pm — before or after her working day.

If a tech asks

Saying the price right

“Your profile, your gallery and getting found are free forever. Booking clients through MNC is free for your first month, then $10.99 a month — no commission, no per-booking fee, and your clients are never charged anything.”

Two things never to say: “forever” next to booking, and “no trial” — the offer is a free month. Anything that says free booking without naming the month is the line that gets quoted back at you.
